About us

Every child known, every child growing

Mountain View Primary and Infant School serves families from the Infant department through to Grade 6. Our teachers, administrators and support staff work together so that each student learns, belongs and thrives.

Small, caring classes

Class teachers know their students and their families, and follow each child's progress through the year.

A strong foundation

Mathematics, Language Arts, Reading and Physical Education in every grade, with Integrated Studies, Science and Social Studies as students move up.

Healthy lunches

Students eat from the canteen or the tuckshop, and families on the PATH programme receive lunch at no cost.
