Welcome to Mountain View!

We are a primary and infant school that cares about every child. From the Infant department all the way up to Grade 6, we help our students learn and grow.

Learn More Our Departments

About Our School

  • Caring Teachers

    Our class teachers know their students and work with parents to help every child do their best.

  • Strong Subjects

    Students take Mathematics, Language Arts, Reading and Physical Education. Older students also do Science and Social Studies.

  • School Lunch

    Students can get lunch from the canteen or the tuckshop. Students on the PATH programme get lunch for free.

Our Departments

  1. Infant Department

    For our youngest students. Children learn through play and build their reading, numbers and social skills.

  2. Primary Department

    Grades 1 to 6. Students are tested every term so parents always know how their child is doing.

  3. After School

    After the afternoon register, students can take part in clubs and sports.

Staff Portal

Staff can log in to take attendance, enter grades, print report cards, record lunch orders and more.
